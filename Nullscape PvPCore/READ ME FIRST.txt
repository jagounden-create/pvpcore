PvPCore 2.5.1 - smooth Diamond SMP combat for Nullscape FFA
=============================================================

INSTALL
  1. On the FFA server, DELETE every older PvPCore jar from plugins/
     (1.0.0, 2.0.x, 2.1.x, 2.2.0). They are all called "PvPCore", so only
     one can load. Keep the plugins/PvPCore folder.
  2. Upload plugins/PvPCore-2.5.1.jar from this folder into plugins/.
  3. ALSO update FFACore to 2.24.2 (Desktop\Nullscape FFACore). From 2.24.2
     on, FFACore's own /ffacore pvp panel switches itself off when PvPCore
     is installed, so PvPCore owns all the combat. FFACore 2.24.1 or older
     still fights PvPCore over the shield (see "WHY" below).
  4. Restart the server (not /reload).
  5. In game: /pvpcore  (or /pvpc) opens the menu.
  Your config.yml is upgraded by itself: the old "instant-block" switch is
  removed, the new ones are added, and a pot speed you never changed moves
  from 1.5 to 2.5.

NEW IN 2.5.1 - shield delay now what PvP servers actually run
  SHIELD DELAY IS 120ms, in the 100-150 you asked for. I looked up
  what PvP servers do with the delay vanilla added: they cut it.
  There are whole plugins for it - InstantShield, No Shield Delay,
  Shield Delay Remover - and the Responsive Shields mod ships at 0
  ticks, suggesting 1-2 ticks only to stop people spamming
  block-hits. 220ms was nearly vanilla and feels slow to anyone used
  to axe-and-shield PvP. Your server picks 120 up by itself: a value
  you never changed by hand follows the new default. Steps of 10 in
  the menu, so 100 or 150 is one click away.

  THE SHIELD DOES NOT RESET. Tested directly this time: a shield held
  up for 75 ticks straight, checked every single tick - 0 rewrites, 0
  resets - including someone changing the delay setting halfway
  through the block. It takes the new value the moment it is lowered.

NEW IN 2.5.0 - the stun, rebuilt the way Diamond SMP does it
  THE STUN IS NOW A REAL STUN. Looking up how stuns actually work
  turned up the piece I had wrong. In vanilla, an axe hit that a shield
  blocks still hands the victim a full second of invulnerability - and
  an invulnerable player takes NO KNOCKBACK - so the follow-up click
  that is supposed to launch them does nothing. That is the whole
  mechanic.
  Paper has a switch for exactly this:
      unsupported-settings.skip-vanilla-damage-tick-when-shield-blocked
  It is what the Diamond SMP servers turn on. PvPCore now turns it on
  while Shield Stun is on, and puts it back the way it was if PvPCore
  is ever removed. Nothing for you to edit.
  What this fixes: my old version held the follow-up window open for
  ONE TICK (50ms). A real double-click is two to four ticks apart, so
  it kept missing - that is your "stuns kind of work". Tested: a
  follow-up 3 ticks after the stun now lands with full knockback,
  where before it was swallowed.
  It works for both stun styles: the vertical one (axe, then a quick
  second axe click) and the horizontal one (axe, swap to sword, hit).

  STUNS WHEN YOU BLOCK-HIT. Vanilla refuses to stun if the ATTACKER is
  holding their own right-click - your own shield up, or a Bedrock
  player swinging mid-use. It reads your shield instead of your axe.
  PvPCore now only asks what weapon you swung.

  THEY GO BACK LESS. New Knockback Distance switch, 0.90 - a tenth
  less ground covered than vanilla, so fights stay close. It scales
  where you end up, so knockback resistance still counts. 1.0 = vanilla.
  Change it in /pvpcore or with /pvpcore set knockback-distance 0.95.

  VERTICAL KNOCKBACK is 0.45 (vanilla 0.4) and lifts every hit within
  a block of the floor - stepping, slabs, both ends of a jump. Higher
  than that you really are in the air, where vanilla is kept so air
  combos still work.

  THE POT AUTO-MOVE IS GONE. 2.4.0 slid your next pot into your hand
  after a throw; you asked for that out and you were right - double
  potting proper is click, scroll one slot, click, and that works on
  its own. Tested: slot 0, use, scroll to slot 1, use = both pots
  thrown, both heal. The setting is removed from your config by itself.

  PASF. If you install PaperAttributeSwapFix, PvPCore hands attribute
  swapping to it instead of both plugins writing the same Paper switch.
  /pvpcore status says so when it sees it.

  Tested on a real Leaf 1.21.11 server: 37 of 37 checks, including
  controls that put vanilla's behaviour back and show the follow-up
  being eaten again.

  One thing outside these plugins: Vulcan can still refuse very fast
  clicking (fastuse / autoclicker checks). If pots or stuns feel
  blocked at high click speeds, that is where to look.

NEW IN 2.4.0 - the three things you came back with
  DOUBLE-CLICK POTTING NOW WORKS. The reason it never did is that a
  splash potion is a stack of ONE: the first click throws it and your
  hand is left EMPTY, so the second click has nothing to throw. No
  cooldown was ever involved. Your next pot now slides into that hand
  one tick (50ms) later, exactly as if you had scrolled to it. It is a
  MOVE, never a copy - throw one of three pots and you have two left.
  Switch: Double-Click Pots in /pvpcore (on).

  STUNS: the last gap closed. Vanilla refuses to stun a shield if the
  ATTACKER is holding a right-click of their own - block-hitting with
  your own shield up, or a Bedrock player who can swing mid-use, quietly
  lost you the stun. PvPCore now only asks what weapon you swung, so an
  axe on a raised shield stuns, full stop. The rule is now simply: if
  the players saw an axe hit a raised shield, it stuns.

  VERTICAL KNOCKBACK: more of it, and in more places. It is a number
  now, 0.45 (vanilla is 0.4), and it applies to every hit, not just the
  ones vanilla dropped. It also counts you as "on the floor" anywhere
  within a BLOCK of it - stepping, slabs, the start and end of a jump -
  instead of only when you were about to land. Above that you really are
  in the air, where vanilla is kept so air combos still work. Knockback
  resistance still counts against it. Raise or lower it in /pvpcore
  (right-click / shift+right-click, 0.30 to 0.80).

  Tested on a real Leaf 1.21.11 server: 34 of 34 checks, including
  controls that show the old behaviour with each fix switched off.

NEW IN 2.3.0 - stuns, vertical knockback, pots
  STUNS NOW ALWAYS WORK. They missed for three reasons, all fixed:
   1. PvPCore and FFACore's /ffacore pvp both rewrote the SAME shield,
      one with no delay and one with 220ms. The shield flipped between
      the two all fight long, which made it "reload" and made stuns
      random. FFACore 2.24.2 now steps aside for PvPCore.
   2. A shield still inside its raise delay isn't blocking yet, so an
      axe hit on a shield you could SEE was up did damage instead of
      stunning. Now an axe on a raised shield always stuns it.
   3. The blocked axe hit gave the victim a full second of hit immunity,
      so the fast follow-up hit either did nothing or landed with no
      knockback, depending on ping. Now the stun hit gives no immunity,
      so the follow-up always lands with full damage and knockback, even
      when both clicks arrive in the same tick.
  Still no stun (on purpose): a sword on a shield, or an axe hitting a
  shield from behind.

  VERTICAL KNOCKBACK. The game only gives the upward part of knockback
  when the server thinks you're on the ground, and it hears that from your
  own client, one ping late. Hits right as you land or step gave NO lift.
  PvPCore now checks where you really are, so those hits lift you like any
  other. A player really high in the air keeps normal knockback.
  (If KnockbackSync is ever installed, this switch steps aside for it.)

  SHIELD DELAY is now a number: 220ms (you asked for 200-250). One fixed
  value for everyone, and a shield is never rewritten while it's raised,
  so it can't "reload". 0 = blocks instantly, 250 = vanilla.

  POTS. No pot cooldown exists anywhere in PvPCore: spam-click or
  double-click and every pot throws (tested: 3 pots in one tick, all
  thrown). Fast Pots now burst the SAME tick they're thrown, even at the
  top of a jump (vanilla takes 2 ticks). Pot Accuracy still heals the
  thrower fully.

  All of this was tested on a real Leaf 1.21.11 server with player NPCs:
  25 of 25 checks passed, including two that switch the fix off and show
  the old bug coming back.

  If pots still don't throw fast on the live server, the cause isn't
  PvPCore - check Vulcan (fastuse / autoclicker checks) and any custom
  cooldown in FFACore's cooldowns.yml.

PEARL COOLDOWN - LEFT TO FFACORE
  PvPCore never touches the pearl cooldown. Keep FFACore's old 14 second
  "Ender pearl cooldown" (/ffa serversettings) OFF; /pvpcore warns while
  it is on.

THE MENU
  Left-click a tile          turn it on / off
  Right-click                raise its number
  Shift + right-click        lower its number
  Every change saves to plugins/PvPCore/config.yml and works instantly.
  Needs pvpcore.admin (ops have it).
  Commands: /pvpcore status | reload | toggle <name> [on|off] | set <name> <number>
  e.g. /pvpcore set shield-delay 150

WHAT EACH SWITCH DOES                                           (default)
  SHIELDS
  Shield Usage       shields can be raised at all                         ON
  Shield Delay       how long a raised shield takes to block              ON 120ms
  Shield Stun        axes always stun a raised shield, the follow-up
                     always lands, and for how long                       ON 5s
  Break Sound        both players hear the shield break loud and clear    ON

  PEARLS + POTS
  Smooth Pearls      land where the pearl actually hit                    ON
  No Pearl Damage    pearls stop hurting you on landing                   OFF
  Fast Pots          pots at your feet burst the tick they are thrown     ON
  Pot Accuracy       your own pot always heals you fully                  ON

  HIT REG
  Instant Knockback  hit + knockback sent the moment it lands (Leaf)      ON
  Vertical Knockback every hit lifts you, whatever your ping           ON 0.45
  Knockback Distance how far back a hit throws them, flat             ON 0.9x
  Attribute Swapping swap items as you hit (axe -> sword keeps damage)    ON
  Hit Delay          invulnerability after a hit (vanilla 20 ticks)       OFF

  ARENA
  Health Under Name  "17 (heart)" under every player's name               ON
  Target Health Bar  hit someone and your action bar shows theirs         ON
  XP Clumps          XP bottles mend your armour in one go                ON
  Ban Crystal PvP    end crystals can't be placed, hit or blown up        ON
  Ban Anchor PvP     respawn anchors can't blow people up                 ON

NOTHING TO EDIT IN LEAF OR PAPER
  Instant Knockback and Attribute Swapping flip the server's own settings
  while PvPCore runs and put them back when it stops.
